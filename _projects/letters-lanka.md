Akuru Collective is for any designer, Illustrator, typographer, type designers (and anyone) who are interested in exploring new dimensions of letters. We always encourage new ideas and community participation.

Letters.Lanka is an affiliate project by Chamodi Waidyathilaka, a founding member of Akuru Collective, where we encourage everyone to share new ideas and showcase their skills in lettering, calligraphy, illustration and letter drawings.in an open platform.

Letter.Lanka believe that through such a platform to share and explore new talent

We can pave the way for more varieties and variations in Sinhala fonts as we expand the type minded community in Sri Lanka. Within a few months from it’s initiation Letters.Lanka has been able to raise awareness about the importance of type and typography in our community.

You can send your creative letter idea all year along. You have all the freedom in the world to go wild and crazy. Then, for submissions and more information, click on the link down below.

Happy Lettering!
