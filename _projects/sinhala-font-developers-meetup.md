Sinhala Typographers Meetup was another initiative by Akuru Collective. The event aimed at sharing knowledge among people interested in character creation in Sri Lanka. On this day participants were given the opportunity to raise their problems, questions and ideas concerning typography in Sri Lanka. One such idea was to establish a platform for font designers to successfully and safely release their fonts.

The event took place at W.A. Silva Museum on Sunday, January 27th, 2019 and was hosted by Akuru Collective, Mooniak, Fontlet and W.A. Silva Museum Foundation.
